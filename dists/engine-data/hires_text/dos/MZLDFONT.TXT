Fonts of the DOS Maniac Mansion, Zak McKracken, Loom and Day of the Tentacle Korean text
========================================================================================
(MML*/MMU*, ZAKL*/ZAKU*, LOOML*/LOOMU*, DOTTL*/DOTTU*.SVF; MMKO, ZAKKO, LOOMKO, DOTTKO.MAP)

The .SVF files are bitmap fonts baked (tools/korean/mkfont.py, plans in
tools/korean/scumm-fonts/{mm,zak,loom,dott}[lu].tsv) from three SIL Open Font
License 1.1 fonts. Each is a Modified Version (a rasterised subset). They
carry no font name at all, and neither the file names nor the text inside
them use a Reserved Font Name.

  MML*.SVF, ZAKL*.SVF, LOOML*.SVF, DOTTL*.SVF   (L preset, 1 bpp)
      Neo Dunggeunmo 1.601, Copyright (c) 2017-2021, Eunbin Jeong (Dalgona.)
      <project-neodgm@dalgona.dev>. Licence: OFL.TXT.

  MMU*.SVF, ZAKU*.SVF, LOOMU*.SVF, DOTTU1/2/3/4.SVF   (U preset, 2 bpp: dialogue and verbs)
      NanumGothic Bold, Copyright (c) 2010, NHN Corporation
      (http://www.nhncorp.com). Licence: OFLNANUM.TXT.

  DOTTU0.SVF, DOTTU7.SVF   (U preset, 2 bpp: the large title and credit text)
      Gowun Batang Bold, Copyright 2021 The Gowun Batang Project Authors
      (https://github.com/yangheeryu/Gowun-Batang). Licence: OFLGOWUN.TXT.

The three licence files hold the exact upstream text, copyright line and
Reserved Font Name list included. The fonts are not sold by themselves.
