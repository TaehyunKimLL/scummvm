2. **`SvfFont::plotPixel` forces `alpha = 255` on 8-bit alpha surfaces (`ARGB8888`/`RGBA8888`/`ABGR8888`) and ignores `SvfPlot::a`**
   - **Location**: [svffont.cpp:36,56-58,115](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/scummvm-review-aa/graphics/fonts/svffont.cpp#L36) (compare `TTFFont::renderGlyph` at [ttf.cpp:648-657](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/scummvm-review-aa/graphics/fonts/ttf.cpp#L648-L657))
   - **Scenario**: Replacing alpha blending with `TextCompose::blendPixel` fixed 1-bit `ARGB1555`/`RGBA5551`, but broke 8-bit alpha surfaces. When an engine or GUI widget draws white AA text (`ARGB(255, 255, 255, 255)`) onto a transparent `ARGB8888` surface (`ARGB(0, 0, 0, 0)`) to alpha-blit over a scene, `blendPixel` blends RGB against the transparent black background and forces `alpha = 255` for every `cov > 0` pixel — turning faint `cov = 85` AA edges into **opaque dark gray `ARGB(255, 85, 85, 85)`** and producing a hard dark halo over bright backgrounds. `SvfPlot::a` ([svffont.cpp:36,115](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/scummvm-review-aa/graphics/fonts/svffont.cpp#L36)) is also now dead/ignored, so semi-transparent text colors (`p.a < 255`) draw opaque.
   - **Fix**: Use `TextCompose::blendPixel` (opaque alpha) when `format.aBits() <= 1` (covering RGB565, RGB555, ARGB1555, RGBA5551, XRGB8888, RGB332), and perform alpha-aware compositing (matching `TTFFont::renderGlyph`'s Porter-Duff `over` or blended alpha when `format.aBits() > 1`) on multi-bit alpha surfaces; update `test_svfFontSurfaces` to assert the distinction between 1-bit and 8-bit alpha surfaces.
