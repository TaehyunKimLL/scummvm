### Critical

1. **DMA channel auto-probe blindly reprograms, unmasks, and masks unrelated ISA DMA channels (0, 1, 3)**
   - **Location:** [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:438-484](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L438-L484) (`DOSSOUNDBLASTER_RestartOnChannel`, `DOSSOUNDBLASTER_ProbeDMAChannel`)
   - **Failure scenario:** On a real Pentium DOS PC where another ISA device owns DMA 0 or DMA 3 (e.g., ECP parallel port, ISA SCSI/tape controller, GUS, or the second DMA channel of a YMF719/OPL3-SA3/WSS card), if `BLASTER` has a wrong IRQ (e.g., `I7` instead of `I5`) or a wrong DMA (`D1`), the initial `WaitForIRQ` times out. `DOSSOUNDBLASTER_ProbeDMAChannel` then loops over `{0, 1, 3}`, overwriting each channel's 8237A mode, page, base address, and count registers via `DOSSOUNDBLASTER_ProgramDMA`, unmasking it for `>= 250 ms` (up to seconds at low sample rates / large buffers) in auto-init memory-to-device read mode, and leaving every probed channel masked (`outportb(0x0A, 0x04 | hidden->dma_channel)`). Any other device armed or active on that channel gets fed audio-buffer silence bytes and is permanently left masked/disabled.
   - **Fix:** Before touching any other DMA channel, sample the configured channel's 8237A current count register (`configured * 2 + 1`) twice across a short delay while playback is active: if the count decrements, the DMA channel is already correct and the IRQ is wrong—do not probe other DMAs. If the configured channel is stalled, read the 8237A status register (`inportb(0x08)`, bits 4–7 = DREQ0..DREQ3) while the DSP is playing to detect which candidate channel (excluding 2) the SB is actually requesting, and only reprogram that channel.

2. **`CheckForSoundBlaster()` waits ~0.3 ms instead of 300 ms (ns/us unit bug), and `ResetSoundBlasterDSP()` can under-wait the 3 us reset pulse**
   - **Location:** [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:45-52](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L45-L52), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:535](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L535), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:728-748](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L728-L748) (called by new code at `:440` and `:705`)
   - **Failure scenario:** `SDL_DelayPrecise()` takes **nanoseconds**, not microseconds. At `:735`, `SDL_DelayPrecise(1000)` requests 1000 ns = 1 us (`target_ticks = (1000 * 1193180) / 1e9 = 1` PIT tick = 0.838 us in [SDL_systimer.c:53](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/timer/dos/SDL_systimer.c#L53)), so 300 iterations wait only ~0.25–0.3 ms instead of the intended 300 ms. On real SB/clone/PnP cards (especially when exiting high-speed DMA in `CloseDevice:705` or resetting between DMA probe retries in `RestartOnChannel:440`), DSP reset takes > 0.3 ms and `CheckForSoundBlaster()` falsely fails—either aborting `OpenDevice` or leaving the card wedged in high-speed mode on close. Similarly, `ResetSoundBlasterDSP()` (`:50`) passes `3000` ns (truncating to 3 PIT ticks, i.e., ~1.7–2.5 us depending on phase), violating the SB datasheet's >= 3 us reset pulse requirement; and `OpenDevice:535` calls `ResetSoundBlasterDSP()` without waiting for `0xAA`, leaving `0xAA` unread in `base+0xA` and risking sending `0xD1` before the DSP is ready.
   - **Fix:** In `CheckForSoundBlaster()`, poll with a real deadline (`SDL_GetTicksNS() + SDL_MS_TO_NS(300)`) sleeping `SDL_DelayPrecise(SDL_US_TO_NS(100))` or `SDL_MS_TO_NS(1)`, draining stale non-`0xAA` bytes until `0xAA` or timeout. In `ResetSoundBlasterDSP()`, wait `SDL_US_TO_NS(10)` (safe margin above 3 us). In `OpenDevice:535`, call `CheckForSoundBlaster()` instead of raw `ResetSoundBlasterDSP()`.

3. **Unvalidated `BLASTER` DMA channel (`D`/`H`) causes out-of-bounds array reads, writes to port `0x00`/arbitrary I/O ports, or clobbering floppy DMA 2 / cascade DMA 4**
   - **Location:** [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:237-242](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L237-L242), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:312-346](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L312-L346), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:538-543](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L538-L543), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:786-793](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L786-L793)
   - **Failure scenario:** `IsSoundBlasterPresent` accepts any `num >= 0` for `D`, `H`, and `I`, and `OpenDevice` only checks `hidden->dma_channel < 0`. On an SB16 with `BLASTER` specifying `H1` or `H3` (a common real-world configuration to avoid DMA conflicts), `high_page_ports[channel]` is `0`, so `outportb(0, physical_page)` writes to DMA channel 0's address register, `0xC0 + (channel - 4) * 4` writes to unrelated ports (`0xB4`/`0xBC`), and `0xD4, channel & ~4` unmasks the wrong high DMA channel. With `H4`, `0xD4` masks/unmasks cascade channel 4 (breaking all low DMA 0–3). With `D2`, `ProgramDMA` reprograms and unmasks the floppy controller's DMA channel. With `D >= 4` or `H >= 8`, `page_ports[channel]` / `high_page_ports[channel]` reads out of bounds and writes to an arbitrary I/O port.
   - **Fix:** Validate `soundblaster_dma_channel` is in `{0, 1, 3}` (never 2 or >= 4), validate `soundblaster_highdma_channel` is in `{5, 6, 7}` (failing cleanly or falling back to 8-bit mode if not), and validate `soundblaster_irq` is in `0..15` (and != 2). Use `0x04 | (channel - 4)` when masking high DMA channels on port `0xD4`.

4. **Missing `"memory"` clobber in `DOS_DisableInterrupts()` / `DOS_EnableInterrupts()` breaks compiler ordering around `cli`/`sti` critical sections**
   - **Location:** [SDL_dos.h:144-152](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/core/dos/SDL_dos.h#L144-L152); callers at [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:293-305](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L293-L305), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:445-448](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L445-L448), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:678-689](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L678-L689)
   - **Failure scenario:** `DOS_DisableInterrupts()` and `DOS_EnableInterrupts()` omit the `"memory"` clobber. Under GCC `-O2`:
     - In `DOSSOUNDBLASTER_RestartOnChannel:445-448`, neither `hidden->dma_channel` nor `isr_dma_channel` (`:114`) is `volatile`, so GCC can move `isr_dma_channel = channel` outside `cli`/`sti`.

     - In `DOS_SBGetStats:295-304`, `isr_chunk_size` is non-volatile, and dereferencing unlocked caller out-pointers while `IF = 0` risks a page fault with interrupts disabled under CWSDPMI.

5. **Signed 32-bit overflow (UB) in `isr_ring_read` / `isr_ring_write` after ~3.38 hours of playback**
   - **Location:** [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:106-107](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L106-L107), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:177-184](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L177-L184), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:220-221](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L220-L221), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:298](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L298), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:687-688](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L687-L688), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.h:41-42](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.h#L41-L42)
   - **Failure scenario:** `ring_read` and `ring_write` are monotonic signed `int` byte offsets. At 44100 Hz S16LE stereo (`176,400 bytes/s`), `ring_write` hits `INT_MAX` (`2,147,483,647`) after 3.38 hours (realistic in a long ScummVM session). `hidden->ring_write += buffer_size` and the subsequent `isr_ring_write - isr_ring_read` subtraction across the wrap boundary invoke signed integer overflow UB; under `-O2`, GCC can miscompile `avail >= isr_chunk_size` or `(size - used) >= chunk_size`, stalling audio or triggering continuous underruns.
   - **Fix:** Use `Uint32` for `isr_ring_read`, `isr_ring_write`, `hidden->ring_read`, and `hidden->ring_write`. Compute `const Uint32 avail = isr_ring_write - isr_ring_read;` and `const Uint32 used = hidden->ring_write - isr_ring_read;`, and check `used <= (Uint32)(size - hidden->chunk_size)` in `WaitDevice`. Because `ring_size` is a power of 2 dividing $2^{32}$, unsigned wraparound is exact and UB-free indefinitely.

6. **`DOS_AllocateDMAMemory()` fails to avoid 64 KB physical boundaries when `len > 32768` (allowed up to 64 KB by `OpenDevice`)**
   - **Location:** [SDL_dos.c:69-88](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/core/dos/SDL_dos.c#L69-L88), called from [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:523-525](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L523-L525) and [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:544-545](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L544-L545)
   - **Failure scenario:** `DOSSOUNDBLASTER_OpenDevice:523` allows `device->buffer_size <= 32768`, making `hidden->dma_buflen` up to `65536` (`0x10000`). If `len = 65536` (or `49152`) and `DOS_AllocateConventionalMemory(len * 2)` returns `physical = 0x18000`, the first half `[0x18000, 0x28000)` crosses `0x20000`, so `ptr += len` shifts to `[0x28000, 0x38000)`, which **still crosses the 64 KB boundary at `0x30000`**. On real 8237A hardware, the DMA address wraps to `0x20000` instead of entering page 3, playing unrelated DOS conventional memory as audio. (Pre-existing in `SDL_dos.c`, but directly breaks real hardware whenever `buffer_size > 16384`.)
   - **Fix:** Check `(physical >> 16) != ((physical + len - 1) >> 16)` (also fixing the off-by-one when `physical + len` lands exactly on a boundary), and when it crosses, advance `ptr` to the next 64 KB boundary (`ptr += 0x10000 - (physical & 0xFFFF);`), which always fits inside the `2 * len` allocation for `len <= 65536`. Reject `len <= 0 || len > 65536`.

7. **`DOS_LockIRQCode` does not check `RingCopyOut` / `ISR_GetDMAHalf`, and DPMI-locked `hidden->ring_buffer` is leaked on every close or open failure**
   - **Location:** [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:612-635](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L612-L635), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:246-248](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L246-L248)

     - `handlers[]` at `:612` only checks `SoundBlasterIRQHandler`, omitting `RingCopyOut` and `ISR_GetDMAHalf`. When not inlined (e.g., `-O0`/`-Os`), if either helper lands outside `[dosIrqBegin_sb, dosIrqEnd_sb]`, `DOS_LockIRQCode` succeeds without locking it, causing a fatal page fault in IRQ context.

8. **Pre-SB16 low sample-rate underflow wraps `time_constant` above 212 (triggering high-speed `0x90`), and SB16 sample rate is never clamped**
   - **Location:** [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:381-384](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L381-L384), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:410-411](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L410-L411), [SDL_dosaudio_sb.c:511-513](file:///home/thkim/work/tmp/sdl3-review-p7/src/audio/dos/SDL_dosaudio_sb.c#L511-L513)
   - **Failure scenario:** `OpenDevice:511` only clamps `freq > 22050` for pre-SB16 and never clamps SB16.
     - On a pre-SB16 mono card opened at `freq = 3800 Hz`, `256 - (1000000 / 3800) = -7`, which casts to `Uint8` as `249`. Since `249 > 212`, `hidden->highspeed` becomes `true`, sending DSP command `0x90` (unsupported on DSP 1.x / 2.00) at ~142.8 kHz.
     - On an SB16 opened at `48000 Hz` (common SDL3 default) or `> 65535 Hz`, `0x41` is sent an out-of-spec rate (SB16 hardware range is 5000–44100 Hz; `> 65535` silently truncates in `(Uint8)(freq >> 8)`), and `freq * SDL_AUDIO_FRAMESIZE` in `ProbeTimeoutMS:418` can overflow `int` for huge values.
   - **Fix:** Clamp `device->spec.freq` in `OpenDevice` to `[5000, 44100]` for SB16, and for pre-SB16 clamp so `effective_rate = channels * freq` stays in `[4000, 44100]` (with `freq <= 22050`). Also gate `hidden->highspeed` on DSP >= 2.01 (`soundblaster_version > 2 || (soundblaster_version == 2 && soundblaster_version_minor >= 1)`).
